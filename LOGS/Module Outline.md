# Hardware, Network and Behaviorist AI — Module Outline

## 1. Intended Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

1. Understand the fundamental concepts of computer hardware, networking, and behaviorist AI, including their roles in modern intelligent systems.

2. Explain the principles of computer architecture, digital logic, processors, memory, and AI hardware accelerators.

3. Describe how AI systems use CPUs, GPUs, TPUs, NPUs, and other specialized hardware for training and inference.

4. Explain the fundamentals of computer networking, including network architectures, protocols, IP addressing, and data communication.

5. Understand how hardware and network infrastructure work together to support AI applications, cloud computing, and distributed systems.

6. Describe the principles of behaviorist AI, including stimulus-response models, reinforcement, rewards, penalties, and learning through interaction.

7. Explain how reinforcement learning enables AI agents to learn from their environments and improve decision-making.

8. Evaluate the performance of AI systems based on computational efficiency, processing speed, latency, bandwidth, scalability, and energy consumption.

9. Identify the limitations, challenges, and ethical considerations of AI systems, including hardware constraints, network security, privacy, bias, and responsible AI development.

10. Apply knowledge of hardware, networking, and behaviorist AI to analyse, design, and evaluate intelligent systems that interact effectively with their environments.

## 2. Prerequisite Knowledge
- Basic computer literacy.
